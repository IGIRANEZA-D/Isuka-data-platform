# Data and metrics

The public summaries use NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey source files covering 2020–2025. Processing and weighting follow the existing Isuka aggregation services; Ask Isuka reuses those verified aggregate outputs and does not create respondent-level outputs.

## Ask Isuka units

- Production: survey-weighted kilograms converted to tonnes.
- Cultivated area: survey-weighted hectares.
- Yield: weighted production divided by weighted cultivated area, in tonnes per hectare.
- Recorded loss rate: weighted recorded loss divided by weighted production. It describes reported loss and is not total waste.
- Loss by stage: confirmed kilogram stage totals from the existing Loss Planner aggregate, converted to tonnes; the displayed percentage is each stage's share of recorded loss.

Cells backed by fewer than 30 underlying survey records are suppressed. Missing combinations remain unavailable rather than being represented as zero. Survey responses are self-reported. Refer to the data-quality metadata on existing dashboard endpoints for source-specific limitations.
